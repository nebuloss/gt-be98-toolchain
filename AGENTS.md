## Gotchas

- The crosstools expect to live under `…/am-toolchains/brcm-arm-hnd/`. The tarball
  preserves that layout (`brcm-arm-hnd/<crosstools-…>`).
- Don't commit the *extracted* toolchain — only the tarball (or, preferably, the
  Release asset). `.gitattributes` LFS-tracks `*.tar.gz|zst|xz`.
- Regeneration needs a full `gt-be98-firmware` build present (the source
  toolchain is fetched by its `tools/fetch-toolchain.sh`).
